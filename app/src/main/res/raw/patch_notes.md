**Meet Jarn**
- Jarn is the new home for your games and apps, built for the controller and both screens
- Your games live on the top screen, and everything you can do with them lives on the bottom screen
- Classic players see a short Meet Jarn preview, and can try Jarn with one press
- Your games, favorites, canvas pages and widgets come with you, nothing to set up again
- Jarn can find your ES-DE games folder and use your existing ES-DE or RetroHrai box art
- Make Jarn your Home in one step from the welcome screen
- A quick tour shows every button, and you can replay it or Meet Jarn anytime from Settings
- Switch back to Classic anytime from Settings, nothing is lost
- Also works on single-screen devices, with the bottom screen opening as a panel

**Controls**
- A opens, B goes back, Y searches and Start opens Settings
- X switches between screens, and holding X swaps them
- Select shows options for whatever is highlighted
- L1 and R1 always change top screen tabs, L2 and R2 always change bottom screen tabs
- Button hints along the bottom show what each button does on that screen
- Choose which screen keeps the controller after you tap one
- A subtle cue shows which screen you just moved to, with a choice of Pulse, Screen dim or Tab mark

**Library and Browsing**
- Browse Systems, Favorites and Recent on the top screen
- Pick Grid or Carousel for systems and games, including a new Shelf carousel
- Games and systems lift when highlighted, with clearer focus borders and bigger system cards
- Smooth transitions when moving between systems and games, and between tabs
- Continue playing banner jumps straight back into your last game
- Choose the emulator, RetroArch core and art for each game or system
- Add up to two companion apps that open beside a game
- Manage systems, hide games and refresh your library from one place
- Guided scraper setup for ScreenScraper and SteamGridDB, all from the controller
- Option to hide system names and game counts
- Favorites and per-game choices are kept when a system rescans

**Now**
- The bottom screen shows big art, stats and recently played games for whatever you highlight
- Resume poster gets you back into the game you were playing
- Random and Last Played buttons, each can be turned off in Settings
- Optional system logo, game count and last played date
- A cover wall fills in for systems with little art
- Play count and play time are tracked for games launched from Jarn

**Canvas**
- Your Canvas pages from Classic work in Jarn, with every tile, widget and photo
- Move, resize and remove tiles with the controller or touch, with undo for every change
- Hold an empty spot to add something there, and drag apps into folders
- Change pages by swiping, dragging a tile to the edge, or pushing the D-pad past the edge
- See the grid, a ghost of the tile and its size while moving
- Choose what the Frontend art tile shows, with padding and an optional clear card
- Pick a page transition, including Classic's gentle page animations
- A hands-on tour teaches Canvas the first time you open it

**Apps and Search**
- A new Apps drawer with pinned, all and hidden apps
- Search now finds apps as well as games and systems
- A controller keyboard for search and every text field
- Dock, app folders, icon packs and app name options are all in Jarn
- Option to hide app names

**Looks and Wallpapers**
- Pick a look for both screens, with a live preview
- Match Command Panel colors so Jarn fits your device theme
- Three live wallpapers: Starstave, Ironweave and Hearthrise
- Choose an image or GIF wallpaper for each screen
- Live wallpapers pause during games, on Battery Saver and with the screen off
- Wallpapers and GIFs still move when Android animations are turned off

**Sound and Extras**
- Background music from a folder or a single track
- Game jingles with GitHub and local packs, and you can create your own
- RSS feeds and players work with the controller
- System Monitor, crash logs, recent apps and FAQ are in Utilities

**Settings, Backups and Updates**
- Redesigned Settings with controls below and a live preview above
- Restoring a backup shows what will change before anything is applied
- Backups now keep looks, icons, RSS feeds, jingles and your Jarn choices
- Restoring no longer loses photos or breaks widgets
- Folders that can't be read are kept safe through backup and restore
- Check for updates and opt into test builds from Settings

**Home Button**
- Home opens Jarn when it's your Home
- During a game, Home keeps the game on screen and brings Jarn up on the other screen
- The bottom screen comes back if Android closes it

**Classic Fixes**
- Removing or deleting a folder no longer leaves a missing tile on the Canvas
- Widgets that are briefly unavailable are no longer removed
- Removing a tile no longer breaks a widget used somewhere else
- Folder data that can't be read is never overwritten
- Hidden builds keep Jarn out of Recents too
