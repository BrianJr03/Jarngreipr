## ES-DE (EmulationStation Desktop Edition)

Jarngreipr bundles two unmodified files from ES-DE so it can read the emulators picked in ES-DE:

- `app/src/main/assets/esde/android/es_systems.xml` (from `resources/systems/android/es_systems.xml`)
- `app/src/main/assets/esde/android/es_find_rules.xml` (from `resources/systems/android/es_find_rules.xml`)

Source: https://gitlab.com/es-de/emulationstation-de, tag v3.5.0,
commit 50e4b600ae533d772bae3ff880d11a09b05dbe84.
